There is a point where writing one more line by hand becomes fake honesty.

If I am checking a symbolic identity, poking at a recurrence, or trying to understand whether a pattern is real or just visually persuasive, I would rather let SageMath do the boring part and keep my attention for the part that actually matters.

That is the plan for this blog too: text when text is enough, computation when it is not.
