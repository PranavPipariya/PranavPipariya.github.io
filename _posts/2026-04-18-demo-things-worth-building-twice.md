I trust an idea more after rebuilding it from scratch.

The first version is where the fantasy lives. The second version is where the constraints show up, the abstractions get embarrassed, and the real shape of the thing becomes visible.

So a lot of what will land here is probably going to be that second-pass energy: clearer, meaner, and less impressed by its own first draft.
